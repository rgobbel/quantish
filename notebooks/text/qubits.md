Every quantish circuit is also a quantum circuit. Give each particle two qubits: a sign qubit (|0⟩ for plus, |1⟩ for minus) and a position qubit (which of a gate's two switch wires it is on). A Fredkin gate at measurement angle θ is then a rotated CNOT: rotate the sign into the θ basis, let the position record the sign (a CNOT from sign to position: the parallel component passes straight, the perpendicular component crosses over), and rotate back. The four split components cos²θ, i·sinθcosθ, sin²θ, −i·sinθcosθ are the matrix entries of that product. A control particle present adds one more NOT on the position, conditioned on where the control particle is, and that is the only operation that entangles one particle with another. A phase plate is a phase conditioned on position; a particle that starts on two wires is an Ry rotation of its position qubit; a control-wire traversal is no operation at all. Where two of a particle's paths would land on the same code, an extra flag qubit keeps them apart.
